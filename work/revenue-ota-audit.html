What it found

Demand is there. Rate and channel discipline are not.

Occupancy was within a point of the comp set while ADR sat 6% below it: stacked OTA discounts, a static BAR on sold-out event dates and wholesale rates leaking below the direct price.

The plan

€74k of it needs no new tools

  • Cap the Booking.com discount stack at 19%, Genius and Mobile only.
  • Fix the channel-manager mapping and reconcile inventory across all room types.
  • Close the wholesale leak: test-book, shut sub-agent IDs, add a rate-integrity clause.
  • Replace the flat seasonal BAR with a six-level, pace-driven ladder and an event calendar.
  • Release minimum stays at 7 days out and move the Expedia flexible rate to 48-hour cancellation.
