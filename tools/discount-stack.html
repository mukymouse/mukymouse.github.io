Why stacks surprise you

Discounts don't add up, they multiply: 10%, 10% and 15% become 31.2% off, not 35%. But owners usually plan for one discount, not three, so the guest ends up paying far less than intended.

How to stop the leak

Decide the maximum discount any guest should get, set deals not to combine with other promotions, and keep deep deals for dates that really need them. Review the Promotions page in your extranet monthly.

Read this before acting

This is a simplified model. Whether promotions combine depends on your settings and on the rate plan, and some guests only booked because of the discount, so your real gain is lower than the leak shown. Not affiliated with Booking.com.
